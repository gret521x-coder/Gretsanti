# Credits

## Tiny Swords — Pixel Frog

Most of the art here comes from **Tiny Swords**, a free pack by **Pixel Frog**
(pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords). The pack is offered free for personal and
commercial use; credit is not required, but it is given here anyway.

Each piece was cropped from its sheet, trimmed of empty space and scaled with nearest
neighbour so the pixels stay square.

## `towers/catapult.png` — drawn here

The pack has no siege engine, so this one was drawn pixel by pixel for this project
(frame, wheels, arm, bucket, rock, rope) in the pack's own palette. Do what you like
with it.

## Reference only — not shipped

Look-and-feel references were pulled from the web while designing the monster lineup:
CraftPix's *Medieval Enemy for TD* and *Pixel Medieval Big Enemy* packs (orc / golem
style), and a set of Pinterest tower-progression boards (stone base → tower → roofed
tower). None of those files are stored in this repository; they were only used to decide
what the game should feel like. If you want that exact art, buy the CraftPix packs — they
are the closest match to "orcs and zombies" in this palette.
